# Worked Example Scope — ETC

## Case title
Common Ancestry vs Common Design (human origins) as a single worked example.

## Why this counts as one example (not two)
Both positions are competing scientific/model-level hypotheses about the same target phenomenon (human biological origins), and both are typically mapped onto a single cluster of interpretive claims about Adam/insan/basyar.

## ETC objects (targets)
- `Q`: Qur’anic text (as fixed, mutawatir lafaz) relevant to human creation and genealogical unity.
- `Ti(Q)`: interpretive claims derived from `Q` (e.g., “Adam is the first biological human”, “single-couple bottleneck”, “no biological ancestry”).
- `D`: empirical data from comparative genomics, population genetics, paleontology, etc. (not detailed here).
- `Mj(D)`: scientific models/hypotheses built from D:
  - `Mj_CA`: common ancestry model (with variants).
  - `Mj_CD`: common design model (design/engineering-style explanation; may or may not be framed scientifically depending on formulation).

## ETC goal
Show that most public “Qur’an vs evolution” conflicts are misclassified as `Conflict(Mj(D), Q)` when they are better represented as `Conflict(Mj(D), Ti(Q))`, and then show how constraints bound revision on the interpretive side.

## Non-goals
- Not adjudicating theology.
- Not declaring one model true.
- Not claiming Qur’an is a biology textbook.
